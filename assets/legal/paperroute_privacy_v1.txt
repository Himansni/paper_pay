PaperRoute Privacy Notice — Version 1

PaperRoute stores the account, agency, employee, customer, delivery, billing,
and collection information entered by an agency. The agency owner controls who
may access that agency's tenant data. Access is limited by membership, role,
permissions, and area or customer assignment.

Account email comes from Firebase Authentication. Owner and agency contact
details are stored to operate the service. Consent acceptance is versioned and
timestamped. Customer GPS coordinates are optional and must only be recorded
with the customer's consent.

Do not enter information that is unnecessary for newspaper delivery, billing,
or collection. Contact the agency owner to correct operational personal data.
