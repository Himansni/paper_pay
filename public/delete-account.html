1. Recommended In-App Deletion Path

If you have the PaperRoute mobile app installed, you can delete your account instantly:

Steps in the mobile application:
  1. Open PaperRoute and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile icon or navigate to Account Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Delete Account, enter your password to re-authenticate, and confirm.

3. Data Retention & Anonymization Policy

In accordance with privacy regulations and commercial accounting laws, data is processed upon deletion as follows:

Data Category Action Taken Rationale
Login Credentials & Auth Account Permanently Deleted Authentication records, password hashes, and user tokens are immediately purged from Firebase Auth.
Personal Profile (Name, Phone, Email) Permanently Erased / Anonymized Personal identity is replaced with generic placeholders (e.g. deleted-hash@deleted.paperroute.local).
Invoices & Billing Records Retained in Immutable Ledger Preserved as part of the agency's financial history to maintain accounting reconciliation and ledger integrity.
Historical Payment Receipts Retained (Collector Anonymized) Confirmed financial transaction history is retained with collector references pointing to anonymized staff records.
