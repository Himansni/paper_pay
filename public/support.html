Frequently Asked Questions

1. How are monthly bills calculated in PaperRoute?
Bills calculate customer charges by multiplying active delivery days in the calendar month by the newspaper daily rate. All customer-specific pauses, custom pricing terms, and opening outstanding balances are automatically factored into the final bill.
2. How do UPI collections work?
In Business Settings, configure your agency's UPI Virtual Payment Address (VPA). During payment collection, PaperRoute generates a dynamic UPI QR code. The customer scans and transfers money directly into your bank account. PaperRoute logs the confirmed receipt in your agency ledger.
3. Does PaperRoute track my delivery boys' live GPS location?
No. PaperRoute V1 does NOT access, collect, or track device GPS hardware, background location, or sensor coordinates. Delivery sequence ordering is organized manually by the distributor.
4. How do I delete my account or close my agency?
You can delete your account directly in the app under Account Settings > Delete Account. If you are an Agency Head, archive all active customer routes and remove staff members first, then execute deletion. You can also submit an external request on our Web Deletion Page.
